# Welcome to Skypixel Framework
This is our space to build and improve Skypixel Framework together.

- Read **#rules** before posting.
- Say hello in **#introductions** and meet people in **#general**.
- Open a topic in **#support** for help or **#bug-reports** for reproducible issues.
- Share images, video, audio and documents in **#showcase**. Use spoilers when a preview needs a warning.
- Join **The lounge** to talk, or **Community stage** for hosted discussions.

Your feedback matters. Clear reports and thoughtful conversations make the next release better for everyone.
