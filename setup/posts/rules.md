# Skypixel Framework community rules
Welcome to the home of Skypixel Framework. Build, test, learn and share with care.

## 1. Respect the people behind the profiles
Treat everyone with patience. Harassment, threats, hateful language, personal attacks and sexual content involving minors are not allowed. Disagree with ideas constructively.

## 2. Protect privacy and account access
Never post passwords, claim tokens, API keys, private conversations or someone else's personal information. Ask before recording a call, camera or shared screen.

## 3. Keep each space useful
Use the channel that fits your post. Search before starting a support topic, give it a clear title and include steps to reproduce a bug. Hide sensitive screenshots behind spoilers. Keep public content suitable for the community.

## 4. Share responsibly
No spam, scams, malicious downloads, impersonation or unsolicited advertising. Only share code and media you have permission to distribute. Explain what an attachment contains before asking people to open it.

## 5. Help us test well
Report one issue per forum post, with the device, browser, expected result and actual result. Remove personal information from logs and screenshots. Do not exploit a bug against another member.

## 6. Make room in voice and stage
Avoid disruptive audio and repeated call invitations. Respect the host and use headphones when needed. A moderator may disconnect a participant who disrupts a room.

## 7. Follow moderation decisions
Moderators may remove content, disconnect a call, issue a timeout, kick or ban to protect the community. A timeout blocks messages and access to voice and stage until it ends. Ban notices include the stated reason and duration. Ask for a review calmly; do not evade a restriction with another account.

## 8. Know the team
**Admin** and **Moderator** roles identify community staff. **Tester**, **Contributor** and **Member** recognize participation; they do not grant ownership. The Nexus creator badge is a separate account badge. Only the server carrying the official shield is the official Skypixel Framework community.

Thanks for helping us make this a welcoming place to create.
